## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), supervised by [Prof. Chen Jason Zhang](https://zhangchen.info/). I also work closely with [Prof. Raymond Chi-Wing Wong](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~raywong/). Previously, I earned my M.S. in Data-Driven Modeling from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and my B.S. in Statistics from Chongqing University.

My research focuses on natural language processing for databases, multimodal large language model (MLLM) pre-training data construction, and data agents. Outside research, I enjoy playing basketball. 🏀

**I am seeking exchange or visiting research opportunities in 2027 and long-term research collaborations.** If our interests overlap, feel free to [email me](mailto:zhuoy.wan@connect.polyu.hk).

## Research Interests

- **Natural Language Interfaces to Data:** Text-to-SQL, Text-to-Visualization, and Text-to-OverpassQL.
- **MLLM Pre-training Data Construction:** automated data quality assessment, data acquisition and curation, diversity assurance, noisy data cleaning, and coreset selection.
- **Data Agents & Optimization:** autonomous data agents and mathematical optimization for improving their performance and efficiency.
